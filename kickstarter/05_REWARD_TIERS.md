# 05 — Reward Tiers (v2, 2026-10-20)

**Why v2:**
- The v1 game tiers (€8/€10) were priced below nearly every successful comparable.
- In the 18 campaigns studied (14 §3), the single-copy tier that sells is **€12–20**, and a **limited early bird is
  usually the most-backed tier**.
- Deluxe tiers at €25–40 and group packs carry the average pledge. The median average pledge for funded games in our
  goal band is **$59**.

Source of truth for prices and mix: `03_funding_model.xlsx` → *Rewards*.

**Rules (unchanged):**
- Every reward is the team's own work (K2), so there's no soundtrack tier until an original score is commissioned.
  That's the 250% stretch goal.
- Steam keys only once Reef Run has its own Steam app (S3).
- At most 2,500 beta keys in total; beyond that, beta access comes through free Playtest keys.
- **Late Pledges stay off** until the game is on sale.
- The campaign runs at most 30 days.
- Planned retail price: **€12.99** (v2 assumption, to validate in playtests; v1 assumed €9.99). Plan a Steam launch
  discount so key parity holds.

**Blended average pledge: €37.56** (v1: €28.90). **Backers needed for the €14,000 goal: 373** (v1: 416 for €12,000).
Beta keys owed at goal: about 683, well under the 2,500 cap.

**Why these exact numbers:**
- **€12 limited early bird:** The Boo Croo $12 (limit 250) and Lou's Lagoon €20 (limit 250) were each campaign's #1
  tier. A real cap of 300 creates honest urgency for day 1, which is what the algorithm rewards (guide p. 20).
- **€15 standard:** the common single-copy price (Boo Croo $15, Mech Tech $15, Avaria €16, School Night Séance €17).
- **€29 Founding:** the €25–35 deluxe slot (Springfield $29, All Will Rise €25/€35, Carnival Hunt $25/$35). It's
  where the cosmetics live.
- **€49 Pod of Four:** €12.25 a key. Fat Goblins' $36 4-pack (limit 100) was its #2 tier, and a 4-player game needs
  this tier most.
- **€99 / €199:** a small share of backers, but most of the margin (Carnival Hunt, Rescue Ops and Avaria all show
  this shape).

## 2. Considered and rejected (unchanged reasoning)

- Soundtrack tier: the music is third-party CC0, so no (it unlocks at the 250% stretch).
- Physical rewards: risk and cost with no evidence of demand.
- Design-a-map/task tiers: unbounded scope.
- Add-ons: none. Keep the pledge flow simple.
- Pledge Over Time: off.
