## Key findings

1. **Kickstarter eligibility: resolved.** The creator is resident in the Netherlands (eligible). Individual project in
   EUR, NL fees 5% + 3% + €0.30. Dutch VAT on reward pledges is the main open money question (03 §6).
2. **Steam key rules shape the campaign** (partner.steamgames.com/doc/features/keys):
   - keys can't be promised before the game has confirmed Steam distribution;
   - the campaign must run at most 30 days, with no late pledges before release;
   - beta keys are capped at 2,500.
3. **Fees** (verified): 5% + 3% + $0.30 per pledge (5% + $0.08 under $10). Third-party blogs still say $0.20.
4. **The market:** friend-group proximity-voice games are huge on Steam, and none was crowdfunded. Original
   multiplayer Kickstarters without an audience mostly fail or stay under $25k. Matchmaking-dependent asymmetric PvP
   games decay to <50 CCU.
5. **The positioning:** "Three fish. One shark. It can hear you." A friends-first party game, not competitive PvP.

## Tools (all run from `kickstarter/`)
- `tools/build_funding_model.py`: regenerates `03_funding_model.xlsx` (overwrites manual edits; edit the xlsx *or*
  the script, not both).
- `tools/build_trackers.py`: regenerates both trackers (same caveat).
- `tools/check_workbook.py <xlsx> [cells]`: evaluates every formula with pycel and reports errors (no Excel needed).
  `tools/xlcalc.py` holds the Python 3.14 / numpy 2 patches pycel needs.
- `tools/make_visuals.py`: rebuilds all graphics; numbers come from the funding model.
- `tools/make_animatic.py`: rebuilds the animatics and VTT captions (needs ffmpeg).
- `tools/make_calendar.py [YYYY-MM-DD]`: regenerates the dated calendar for a new launch date.
- `tools/fetch_steam_comps.py`: re-pulls the Steam comparables data.
