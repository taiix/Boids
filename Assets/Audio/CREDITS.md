# Audio credits

Every sound and music file in `Assets/Audio` is free for commercial use. All are CC0 (public domain) except
**Acid burn**, which is **CC-BY 3.0** and must be credited. The game credits every author anyway, on the
main menu's Credits screen (`Assets/Scripts/Audio/GameCredits.cs`; keep the two in step).

Downloaded 2026-10-15 (the dark round tracks and the deep ambience on 2026-10-21). Conversions: WAV/FLAC files were
re-encoded to Ogg Vorbis; the 2026-10-21 files were also level-matched (volume only), and Water/AmbientDeep was
slowed to 80% and low-passed to make it darker.
