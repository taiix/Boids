## 4. What is REEF RUN?

REEF RUN is an online game for **four players** on PC (Steam) **(built)**. Every round, one player becomes a
**great white shark** and the other three become **small reef fish** **(built)**.

- **The fish** have to survive a 10-minute clock **(built)**. They can **vanish into a real school of fish**: swim
  into it and you shrink to their size and move with the flock, and the shark has to work out which one is you
  **(built)**. Co-op **tasks** around the reef take time off the clock **(built)**. When it hits zero, a wall of the
  arena lifts. Now the shark can see everyone, and the fish have to make a run for it **(built)**.
- **The shark** hunts with bites, a long lunge and a **sonar pulse** that reveals any fish that's moving **(built)**.
  And it **hears** them.

## 5. The twist: the shark can hear you

You talk to your team on **proximity voice**: voices carry only so far underwater **(built)**. The tasks *need*
talking. To free a turtle tangled in a ghost net, each fish sees a different word at its rope, and two ropes have to
be bitten at the same moment **(built)**. But any fish that speaks within the shark's earshot shows up on the shark's
screen as a **ripple**: pin-sharp up close, vague at the edge of hearing **(built)**. A fish heard clearly can be
locked onto **(built)**, and its HUD warns it: **SHARK HEARD YOU** **(built)**.

So every round becomes a negotiation between *we have to talk* and *we have to shut up*.

## 6. The core loop

1. **Lobby:** invite three friends (or open a public lobby). The host picks the clock length **(built)**.
2. **Roles:** one shark, three fish **(built)**.
3. **Survive:** hide in schools, eat to stay strong, dodge the sonar **(built)**.
4. **Co-op tasks:** rescue the turtle, repeat the sequence, sink the trash. Each one takes time off the clock
   **(built)**.
5. **The reef fights back:** mid-round events like a sweeping trawler net, nightfall, a red tide, a jellyfish swarm,
   a storm, a cold layer, a sand slide or a current **(built: 8 events)**.
6. **Escape:** the wall lifts, everyone's revealed, swim for the gap. The fish win if more than half escape **(built)**.
7. **Eaten?** Spectate, and talk freely with the other dead players on their own voice channel. Once per round, a
   ghost can flash the shark's position to the living fish **(built)**.

## 14. Risks and challenges

> **Our biggest risk is our own size.** We're two developers. If one of us becomes unavailable, development slows
> down. We've scoped the goal to what exists plus polish, so there are no big new systems to invent.
> **Online games are hard.** Real-world networks break things a local test doesn't. That's why backers get a beta
> months before release, and why bug fixing is the biggest line in our plan.
> **REEF RUN needs four players.** It's built for friend groups. Public lobbies and our Discord help solo players
> find a group, but we're not promising bots or smaller modes.
> **PC performance.** The reef is rendered in Unity HDRP, which is demanding. We'll publish minimum specs from real
> tests before the beta, and Low/Medium presets are part of the funded work.
> **Dates can move.** Our release window already has a 30% time buffer. If a date moves anyway, you'll hear it from us
> in an update first, with the reason and the new date.
