# 08 — Pre-launch Marketing Plan

**Starting point (verified):** zero audience, zero budget committed, a two-person team (00). The plan relies on
the one thing the game does that others don't (*voice gives you away*) and puts it in front of people as often and
as cheaply as possible. The schedule assumes the validation gates (02 §9) pass. **If they don't, phases C–F don't
happen.**

**Rules for every channel:**
- Never post without the team's approval. Nothing here has been sent or published.
- No bots, no purchased followers, no fake reviews, no sock-puppet accounts. Kickstarter forbids inflating backer
  numbers (K2).
- Disclose that you're the developer in every community post.
- Follow each community's self-promotion rules (read them every time; they change).
- Label paid placements and any free keys given to creators.
- No deceptive urgency or fake scarcity. The only limited tiers are the two real caps in 05.

## 2. Announcement strategy

1. **Soft announce** (phase A, Steam page live): one post per platform with V1 (the whisper clip), plus a devlog
   "We're two devs making a shark game where talking gets you eaten."
2. **Playtest announce** (each playtest): a Discord event + Post 3 (02 §5) in communities that allow playtest calls.
3. **Next Fest announce:** a dated graphic and a demo trailer (a 45 s cut of the campaign video).
4. **Kickstarter announce** (pre-launch page live): "We're launching on Kickstarter on April 13. Follow to be
   notified." Pin it everywhere.
5. **Launch:** see §5.

## 4. Kickstarter pre-launch page strategy

- It becomes available only after the project passes review (K11). **Submit 4+ weeks before launch**, to leave time
  for edits.
- Page content: the project image, the 30 s cut, 3 bullets, the honest status, and the launch date.
- **One CTA everywhere for 4 weeks:** "Notify me on launch" (the link replaces Steam links in bios during phase E).
- **Two pre-launch updates** (they email followers): week −3 "Here's the Pod of Four" and week −1 "Launch time +
  first-48-hours plan".
- **Track daily** in `11_campaign_dashboard.xlsx` → *Pre-launch*. **Postpone if the expected launch backers are
  under 30% of the goal one week out** (trigger T1).

## 6. Mid-campaign (days 3–26)

Funding usually slows in the middle (an assumption from common crowdfunding patterns; see the Forecast sheet).
Planned content, one item every 2–3 days:
- Update: "How the hearing mechanic works", with a dev clip.
- Update: "Shark balance: what playtesters told us".
- A creator match night (stream with 3 viewers vs the dev shark).
- Tier spotlight: Pod of Four. "Bring your group."
- A behind-the-scenes look at how the boids school works.
- **No new limited tiers. No surprise stretch goals.** The only milestone is the costed €53,000 (05 §4).
- Ads: run only while the ad-attributed cost per backer stays below the net revenue per backer (trigger T6).

## 7. Final 72 hours

- Kickstarter emails followers 48 hours before the end (a platform feature, observed; not verified for 2027).
- Final email (template E5) and a last-48h update with honest numbers.
- A final livestream in the last 6 hours.
- The message is a real deadline ("the campaign ends Thursday at 15:00 CET; keys afterwards only at the Steam
  release"). That's true because Late Pledges stay off (S3).

## 9. Creator outreach

- **Who:** creators who already stream proximity-voice friend games or asymmetric games, 500–20k followers, an
  active chat; plus shark and ocean-game YouTubers (S2).
- **How:** a personal message (template C1), a free playtest key with no conditions, a press kit link, and the offer
  of a dev to fill out the lobby if they lack a fourth player. Never pay for a positive review. If a placement is
  paid, both sides disclose it.
- **Track:** name, channel, size, date, response, coverage link, wishlists spike (UTM) in the *Weekly KPIs* notes or
  a separate tab.

## 10. Press outreach

- **The angle:** "A shark game where your voice chat is how you die." Two waves: Next Fest (demo) and Kickstarter
  launch.
- **The list:** about 30 writers who've covered friend-group co-op games or asymmetric multiplayer in the last 12
  months (build it from their bylines). Personal email, template P1, embargo-free. A press kit page with screenshots,
  the trailer, a fact sheet and contact.
- **Expectation:** low hit rate for an unknown team; no plan depends on press.
