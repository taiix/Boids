# 10 — Outreach Templates

**Nothing here has been sent.** Each template is a starting point. Personalise every message: a creator or writer
who gets an obvious mass email ignores it. `[[…]]` = fill in. Don't claim coverage, quotes, numbers or partners
that don't exist. Disclose free keys and any payment. Respect unsubscribe requests and each platform's anti-spam
rules. Kickstarter's rules also forbid unsolicited spam (K2).
