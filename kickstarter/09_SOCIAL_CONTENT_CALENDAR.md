# 09 — Social Content Calendar

Format: 9:16 vertical, 12–35 s, captions burned in, the hook in the first 1.5 s, **real gameplay and real voices
only**, with consent from everyone recorded. One edit goes to TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels; native
posting beats cross-post watermarks. Cover template: `assets/social/social_vertical_cover_1080x1920.png`.

**CTA by phase:**
- Validation (phase B): "Wishlist on Steam" or "Join the playtest".
- Pre-launch (phase E): "Notify me on Kickstarter".
- Campaign (phase F): "Back it, link in bio".

The `[CTA]` cell below means: use the CTA of the phase in which the clip is posted.

Funnel stages: **Awareness** (strangers) → **Interest** (what is this?) → **Consideration** (is it for me and my
group?) → **Conversion** (wishlist, follow, back) → **Community** (keep backers and players engaged).

## 2. Weekly rhythm

- **Mon:** awareness clip (comedy or spectacle).
- **Wed:** interest or consideration clip (mechanic, task, dev story).
- **Fri:** the best performer of the last 2 weeks re-cut with a new hook (A/B), or a conversion clip in phases E–F.
- **Sat:** Discord game night (from the playtest phase onward).
- **Every 2 weeks:** Steam event / devlog. **Monthly:** email newsletter.
