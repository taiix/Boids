Three fish. One shark.
It can hear you.

REEF RUN is an online party game for 4 friends. Hide inside a school of a thousand fish, finish co-op tasks, and swim for the exit. But you're on proximity voice, and the shark sees who's talking.

Subscribers get playtest invites before public sign-ups, and a 4K wallpaper pack. The playtest is free. No spam; unsubscribe anytime. Privacy

Wishlist on Steam

Hide in the school

Swim into a school and you shrink to its size and move with the flock. The shark has to read behaviour, not shapes.

Talk, carefully

Tasks need teamwork: each fish sees a different clue. Every word you say near the shark shows up on its screen.

Or be the shark

Bite, lunge, sonar, and very good hearing. Three friends think they're hiding from you.

Where the game is: REEF RUN is playable and in development by a two-person team. Online rounds, voice, tasks and world events work today. We're now fixing bugs and polishing for public playtests (2026–2027). PC (Windows) on Steam. Dates and features may change; we'll say so here first.
